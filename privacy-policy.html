This Privacy Policy describes how Block Puzzle Stack (the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our App. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Overview

Block Puzzle Stack is an offline casual puzzle game. We do not require or support any account registration or login, and we do not collect, upload, or store any personal information on our own servers. The App contains no user-generated content and no social features.

The only third-party service integrated into the App is Google AdMob, a mobile advertising service, which may automatically collect certain device-level information as described below.

2. Information Collected by Third Parties (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements (including rewarded video ads). As the advertising provider, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers — the Google Advertising ID (GAID) on Android, used to show ads and measure ad performance;
  • Device information — device model, operating system version, screen size, and language settings;
  • Approximate location — derived from IP address, used for regional ad compliance and content;
  • Usage data — ad impressions, clicks, and interaction events related to ad delivery.

This information is collected and processed by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy. We do not control it and have no direct access to it beyond standard AdMob reporting. For details on how Google uses data, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You may opt out of personalized advertising through your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads → Delete/reset advertising ID on Android), or via https://adssettings.google.com.

3. Local Data Storage

The App stores the following data only on your device, using Android local storage (SharedPreferences). This data is never transmitted to us or to any server we operate:

  • Game progress (current level, unlocked levels);
  • Remaining counts of in-game boosters (shuffle / clear);
  • Theme preference and tutorial completion status.

Clearing the App's data or uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of this local data.

4. Permissions

The App requests only the INTERNET and VIBRATE permissions. INTERNET is required to load advertisements; VIBRATE is used solely for optional haptic feedback during gameplay. The App does not access your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or precise location.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children, because the App itself collects no personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can address the issue.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Since all game data is stored locally on your device, it is retained only for as long as the App remains installed. You can delete all local data at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data. Data collected by Google AdMob is governed by Google's data retention practices; you may reset your advertising ID as described in Section 2.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your region (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict its processing. Because we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google as the data processor. For any other inquiry, contact us by email and we will respond within a reasonable timeframe.
